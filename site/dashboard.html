One row per published schedule, since a train's times can differ by day. Delays are measured against each trip's own schedule. Times are local to each station. Click a column to sort.

Source: Amtrak Status Maps (asm.transitdocs.com), which records Amtrak's reported arrival and departure times. Delays use Amtrak's reported actual times. Amtrak seldom posts an actual arrival at a train's last stop (for example New York for the Crescent), so there the final estimate is used once the train has left the stop before. Trips with no reported time are left out of delay stats. Canceled trips are counted separately.
